#+title: Books — the reading list
#+property: header-args:emacs-lisp :tangle "48-books.el"
#+startup: overview

The reading list in =books.org= (=my-booklist-file=): the hook that stamps a
book's dates, rating and comment, and the =C-q l= keys.  Home only.

The rest of the system lives with what it modifies: the =b= capture template in
=40-org.org=, the =cb= view and its comparator in =46-agenda-personal.org=.  The
states (=LEYENDO SIGUIENTE PENDIENTE PAUSADO | LEIDO ABANDONADO=) are defined by
the =#+TODO= line of =books.org= itself, not here: a file-local keyword set
replaces the global one, so they never appear in =tasks.org=.

=books.org= deliberately has no link to org-roam.  Book notes are roam notes
created by hand; reading ideas are captured into the dailies (=C-c n d r=) and
moved into the book's note (=org-roam-refile=) at the daily review, so nothing
stays in the dailies.

* Dates, rating and comment on state change
Only =books.org= is touched.  =FECHA_INICIO= is written once, so resuming a
=PAUSADO= book keeps the original start.  =FECHA_FIN= repeats the =CLOSED:=
line on purpose: properties are what column view and the =cb= comparator read.
The reason for =PAUSADO= and =ABANDONADO= is asked for by the =@= in their
keyword definitions, not here.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (defun my/books--in-booklist-p ()
    "Non-nil when the current buffer visits `my-booklist-file'."
    (and buffer-file-name
         (file-equal-p buffer-file-name my-booklist-file)))

  (defun my/books--empty-prop-p (prop)
    "Non-nil when PROP is missing or blank on the entry at point.
  `org-entry-get' returns \"\" for a property written with no value."
    (string-empty-p (or (org-entry-get nil prop) "")))

  (defun my/books-on-state-change ()
    "Stamp the dates, rating and comment of a book when its state changes."
    (when (my/books--in-booklist-p)
      (let ((today (format-time-string "%Y-%m-%d")))
        (pcase org-state
          ("LEYENDO"
           (when (my/books--empty-prop-p "FECHA_INICIO")
             (org-entry-put nil "FECHA_INICIO" today)))
          ("LEIDO"
           (org-entry-put nil "FECHA_FIN" today)
           (org-entry-put nil "VALORACION"
                          (completing-read "Valoración: " '("5" "4" "3" "2" "1") nil t))
           (let ((comment (read-string "Comentario breve (vacío para omitir): ")))
             (unless (string-empty-p comment)
               (org-entry-put nil "COMENTARIO" comment))))
          ("ABANDONADO"
           (org-entry-put nil "FECHA_FIN" today))))))

  (add-hook 'org-after-todo-state-change-hook #'my/books-on-state-change)
#+end_src

* Reading ideas left in the dailies
The daily review should leave no =:read:= entry behind (the tag comes from the
=r= dailies template).  This lists the ones still there; on each, =org-roam-refile=
moves it into the book's note.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (defun my/books-daily-leftovers ()
    "Search the roam dailies for reading ideas not yet moved to a book note."
    (interactive)
    (consult-ripgrep (expand-file-name "daily/" my-roam-dir) ":read:"))
#+end_src

* Keys
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (let ((books (my/key-define-submap "l" "libros")))
    (my/key-define "a" "añadir libro"
                   (lambda () (interactive) (org-capture nil "b")) books)
    (my/key-define "v" "vista de libros"
                   (lambda () (interactive) (org-agenda nil "cb")) books)
    (my/key-define "f" "buscar libro"
                   (lambda () (interactive)
                     (find-file my-booklist-file)
                     (consult-org-heading))
                   books)
    (my/key-define "d" "ideas en diarios" #'my/books-daily-leftovers books)
    (my/key-define "m" "mover a nota"     #'org-roam-refile          books))
#+end_src
