behaviour: the browser and dired, the ebook and feed readers, the PDF viewer,
eshell, mastodon and the dashboard.  None of it is needed at
startup, so anything here that still loads eagerly is a candidate for deferral.

* Buffer grouping

** ibuffer filter groups
Group buffers by project area. The catch-all "Other" group at the end catches anything unmatched.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (when my-homeenvironment-p
    (setq ibuffer-saved-filter-groups
          `(("personal"
             ;; both ~/.emacs.d and the data dir under Nextcloud/config
             ("Emacs config" (filename . "/.emacs.d/"))
             ("Agenda"       (filename . ,(regexp-quote my-agenda-dir)))
             ("Writing"      (filename . ,(regexp-quote my-escritura-dir)))
             ("Org-roam"     (filename . ,(regexp-quote my-personal-roam-dir)))
             ("Blog"         (filename . ,(regexp-quote
                                           (expand-file-name "personal/" my-nextcloud-dir))))
             ("Special"      (or (name . "^\\*")
                                 (mode  . special-mode))))))

    (add-hook 'ibuffer-mode-hook
              (lambda ()
                (ibuffer-auto-mode 1)
                (ibuffer-switch-to-saved-filter-groups "personal")))
    )

  (when my-workenvironment-p
    (setq ibuffer-saved-filter-groups
          `(("work"
             ("Emacs config" (filename . "/.emacs.d/"))
             ;; Must precede "Work agenda": the roam store is a subdirectory of
             ;; it, and ibuffer takes the first matching group.
             ("Org-roam"     (filename . ,(regexp-quote my-work-roam-dir)))
             ("Work agenda"  (filename . ,(regexp-quote my-work-dir)))
             ("Special"      (or (name . "^\\*")
                                 (mode  . special-mode))))))

    (add-hook 'ibuffer-mode-hook
              (lambda ()
                (ibuffer-auto-mode 1)
                (ibuffer-switch-to-saved-filter-groups "work"))))
#+end_src

** elfeed suite

RSS read in emacs.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (when my-homeenvironment-p
    ;; to store our rss feeds sources
    (use-package elfeed-org
      :ensure t
      :defer t)

    (use-package elfeed
      :ensure t
      :commands (elfeed elfeed-update)
      :init
      ;; Plain settings, safe before the package loads -- and
      ;; `rmh-elfeed-org-files' MUST be set before `elfeed-org' runs.  It used
      ;; to be the other way round: `(elfeed-org)' was called in `:init' while
      ;; the file list was only set in `:config', so elfeed-org started with
      ;; its default list.
      (setq-default elfeed-search-filter "@1-months-ago")
      (setq elfeed-db-directory (expand-file-name "elfeed/db/" my-data-dir))
      (setq rmh-elfeed-org-files
            (list (expand-file-name "elfeed/elfeed.org" my-data-dir)))

      :config
      (require 'elfeed-org)
      (elfeed-org))

    ;; Defined outside the `use-package' so that C-q o f is callable before
    ;; elfeed itself is loaded; calling it autoloads elfeed.
    (defun my/elfeed ()
      "Opens elfeed and updates it in just a go."
      (interactive)
      (elfeed)
      (elfeed-update))
    )
#+end_src

* Keymap entries
The =C-q o= sub-map.  Each entry is registered under the same condition that
installs the application itself: elfeed and mastodon are home-only, and
mastodon also needs the encrypted directory mounted, exactly as their blocks
above require.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (let ((online (my/key-define-submap "o" "online")))
    (my/key-define "e" "eww" #'eww online)
    (when my-homeenvironment-p
      (my/key-define "f" "elfeed" #'my/elfeed online)
      (when my-clear-directory-is-mounted-p
        (my/key-define "m" "mastodon" #'mastodon online))))
#+end_src
